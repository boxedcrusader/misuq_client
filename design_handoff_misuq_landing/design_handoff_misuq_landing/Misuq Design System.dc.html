Misuq Design System

The visual language of the marketing copilot.

A single reference for the colors, type, and components used across Capture, Draft review, Report-back, and Graduate. Presented in the system's own vocabulary so it reads like the product, not a template.

01

Color

Four roles. Restraint over range.
Electric Indigo #5B4FE9
Primary accent — CTAs, focus states, active selections.
Deep Ink #1B1830
Text — near-black with a violet undertone, never pure black.
Lavender Ground #F7F6FC
Background — a pale lavender-white tint, not pure white.
Soft Periwinkle #C7C2E8
Secondary — borders, placeholders, de-emphasized elements.
02

Typography

Two families, clear division of labor.
Space Grotesk DISPLAY / PROMPT

Geometric sans with distinct character. Used where personality matters — headlines, display, and the prompts the copilot speaks in.

Inter BODY / UI

Clean and legible at small sizes. Carries body copy, UI labels, chat text, and buttons — everything read rather than felt.

04

Voice & tone

How the whole thing should feel.

Minimal but not cold. Confident and considered, never decorative.

No dashboard chrome. No heavy borders or drop shadows doing decorative work — structure comes from space and hairlines, not boxes.

Generous whitespace. The interface breathes; nothing is crowded to fit more in.

Low-friction and founder-paced. The copilot waits on the founder, not the other way around.

No urgency-driven or countdown UI. No streaks, timers, or manufactured pressure — validation happens when the founder says it's ready.
